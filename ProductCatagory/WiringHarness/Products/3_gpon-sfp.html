      GPON SFP

      Mecwin's GPON OLT SFP Module is specifically designed for passive optical network applications. It provides support for asymmetric data rates, operating at 2.488G downstream and 1.244G upstream. The GPON OLT transceiver adheres to ITU-T G.984.2 standards. It comes in a small form factor pluggable package with an SC receptacle. The digital diagnostic monitoring function is in line with SFP MSA.
