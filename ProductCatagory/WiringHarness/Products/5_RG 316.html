        RG316 N Male to SMA Male

        Key Features

        • Designed for flexibility and compact RF interconnections, offering stable performance in space-constrained installations.
        • Utilizes silver-plated conductors with PTFE dielectric, ensuring reliable signal integrity and thermal stability.
        • Features double shielding (braid + foil) for effective protection against EMI and RFI interference.
        • Lightweight and highly flexible construction suitable for tight routing and dynamic applications.

        Applications

        • Provides secure RF connectivity between antennas and communication equipment, including radios and transceivers.
        • Commonly used in test and measurement setups, RF labs, and instrumentation systems.
        • Ideal for GPS, IoT devices, wireless modules, and telecom applications requiring compact cabling solutions.
        • Supports interconnection of RF modules in embedded and mobile communication systems.
