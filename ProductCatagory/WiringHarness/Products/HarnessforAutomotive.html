Automotive Wiring Harness Solutions

Harness for Automotive

Mecwin offers advanced automotive wiring harness solutions designed for EV, hybrid, and conventional vehicles with reliable connectivity, lightweight design, and long-term durability.

Advanced Harness for Automotive Solutions

We deliver precision-engineered wiring harnesses tailored for diverse industries, ensuring reliable connectivity and durability. Using advanced CAD tools and automated manufacturing, our harnesses meet stringent ISO and IPC/WHMA-A-620 standards.

For the automotive sector, our harnesses are designed to withstand extreme heat, vibration, oil, and moisture while delivering consistent electrical performance. They connect critical systems such as sensors, actuators, ECUs, lighting, infotainment, and powertrain components in electric, hybrid, and conventional vehicles.

Key Features

  • Engineered for EVs, hybrids, and conventional vehicles
  • Resistant to harsh automotive environments
  • Specialized assemblies for mid drive motor UVW, hub motor, battery and cooling systems, and AC/DC charging solutions
  • Compliance with global automotive standards including ISO and IATF 16949
