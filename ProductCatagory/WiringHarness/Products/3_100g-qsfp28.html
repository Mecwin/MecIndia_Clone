        100G QSFP28

        Mecwin's 100G QSFP28 series comprises SR4, CWDM4, LR4, and ER4 lite modules. These modules utilize LC or MPO receptacles and adhere to the QSFP28 MSA standards. Also this product is compliant with IEEE802.3bm standards, offering low power consumption and high reliability. It's well-suited for 100G data center networks and OTN networks.
