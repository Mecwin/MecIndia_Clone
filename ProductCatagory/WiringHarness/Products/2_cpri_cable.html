        Fibre To The Antenna - CPRI

        Product Features

        • Available in Multimode and Single mode versions.
        • Any connector options like FC, SC. LC, ST & Hybrid.
        • Uses high quality ferrules and connectors.
        • Factory terminated and 100% tested for Insertion.
        • Loss and Return Loss.
        • Individual test sheet and unique product identification number for traceability.
        • Manufacturing process qualified to meet end face geometry requirements.
        • Good repeatability; Mutual thrust performance.
        • Available in IP65, IP66, IP67, IP68.

        Applications

        • Data centre & Datacom applications
        • Telecommunications and FTTx
        • CATV
        • Fiber optic test equipment & sensors.
        • Passive optical networks & fiber communication systems.

        Technical Specification

        Type Specification
        Specification Multi-Mode – 62.5/125 (OM1), 50/125 (OM2), OM3, OM4 Single mode – OS1/OS2 9/125µm, (G657a & b), G652D, G657 B3
        Connector Types SC, LC, FC, ST, E-2000, Mini LC – Simplex & Duplex
        End face finish PC, UPC, APC

        Optical Performance

        Type Specification Value
        Insertion Loss (Multimode) SC, LC, FC, ST, E-2000, Mini LC ≤ 0.3 dB
        Insertion Loss (Single mode) SC, LC, FC, ST, E-2000, Mini LC ≤ 0.3 dB
        Return Loss (Multi mode) SC, LC, FC, ST, E-2000, Mini LC ≥ 35 dB for UPC  ≥ 60 dB for APC
        Return Loss (Single mode) SC, LC, FC, ST, E-2000, Mini LC ≥ 50 dB for UPC  ≥ 60 dB for APC
