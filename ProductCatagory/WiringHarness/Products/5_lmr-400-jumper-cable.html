        LMR400 Jumper Cable

        Key Features

        • Engineered for low insertion loss and superior high-frequency performance, ensuring minimal signal attenuation across a wide frequency range.
        • Incorporates multi-layer shielding and a ruggedized outer jacket, providing excellent resistance against electromagnetic interference (EMI), radio frequency interference (RFI), and mechanical stress.
        • Available in customizable lengths to support diverse deployment and installation requirements.

        Applications

        • Facilitates high-efficiency connectivity between antennas and RF devices, including radios and transceivers.
        • Enables stable signal transmission in broadcasting systems, linking transmitters and receivers with minimal loss.
        • Widely used in wireless communication infrastructures, including WLAN, telecom, and RF networking systems.
