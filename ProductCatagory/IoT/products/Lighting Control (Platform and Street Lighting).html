          Lighting Control (Platform and Street Lighting)

          Lighting control systems for platforms and street lighting utilize advanced technologies to automate, monitor, and optimize lighting operations, ensuring energy efficiency, enhanced safety, and improved user experience in public and industrial environments.

          Key features

          • Automated control: Enables scheduled or sensor-based lighting adjustments, such as dimming or turning lights on or off based on time, occupancy, or ambient light levels.
          • Energy efficiency: Reduces power consumption through LED integration, adaptive brightness, and smart control algorithms.
          • Real-time monitoring: IoT-enabled systems provide real-time data on lighting status, energy usage, and maintenance needs via centralized platforms.
          • Improved safety and visibility: Enhances illumination for streets and platforms, improving safety for pedestrians, vehicles, and workers.
          • Scalable smart infrastructure: Supports integration with smart city infrastructure and scalable deployment for small to large-scale lighting networks.
