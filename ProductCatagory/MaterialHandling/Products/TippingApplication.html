    Tipping Application (Electrical Actuator)

    Download brochure
    Download brochure

    Product Overview

    The MECWIN Electrical Actuator for tipping applications is designed to deliver powerful, efficient, and precise lifting for heavy-duty commercial and industrial vehicles. It ensures smooth and controlled tipping operations.

    Engineered to handle loads up to 2 tons, this actuator offers exceptional reliability, low maintenance, and long service life, making it ideal for demanding environments.

    Key Features

    • 1.2 kW power output for strong and efficient performance
    • Operates at 2500 RPM for quick and smooth tipping actions
    • Delivers torque range of 4 Nm to 6 Nm for optimal load handling
    • Supports tipping loads up to 2 tons
    • Durable design for heavy-duty and long-term use
    • Low maintenance and reliable operation in tough environments
