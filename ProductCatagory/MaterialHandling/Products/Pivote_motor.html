          Pivot Motor

          Pivot Motor

          Download brochure

          Overview

          Designed for demanding warehouse automation environments, our Integrated Pivot Actuator delivers high torque and precise angular positioning in an ultra-compact footprint. Built with robust construction and a zero-maintenance design, it integrates a high-performance BLDC motor with a precision planetary gearbox. It is ideal for cross-belt sorters, AGV steering mechanisms, and dynamic pivot modules. Precision engineering ensures smooth directional changes, reduced downtime, and an exceptional service life exceeding 10 million start-stop cycles, even under continuous heavy-duty operations.

          Key Features

          • Ultra-Compact Envelope: Entire motor and gearbox integrated within a 55mm x 55mm profile.
          • High Peak Torque: Achieves up to 9.0 Nm peak torque for rapid, heavy-load directional changes.
          • Zero-Backlash Precision: Planetary gearbox with ≤ 15 arc-min backlash ensures pinpoint accuracy.
          • Long Service Life: Dual ball bearing system engineered for continuous 24/7 bidirectional operation.
          • Plug-and-Play Design: Factory-matched unit with keyed, splined, or D-shaped rotating shaft options to eliminate field assembly.
