    Mecwin Motor for Scissor Lift Applications

    Download brochure
    Download brochure

    Product Overview

    The Mecwin motor for scissor lift applications is designed to deliver powerful lifting performance with precision control. Its robust construction and efficient power delivery make it ideal for demanding industrial environments, ensuring safe, stable, and energy-efficient lifting.

    Key Features

    • High power output of 3 kW for reliable heavy-duty lifting
    • Smooth operation with 2500 RPM speed for controlled movement
    • Strong 12 Nm torque for effective load handling
    • Supports heavy loads up to 5 tons with ease
    • Durable industrial-grade construction for long-term performance
