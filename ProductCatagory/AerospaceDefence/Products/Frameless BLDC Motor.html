        Frameless BLDC Motors

        A frameless BLDC motor is a high-performance brushless motor supplied without a housing, shaft, or bearings, allowing direct integration into the customer’s mechanical assembly. It offers high torque density, precise control, and a compact design, making it ideal for space-constrained and high-precision applications such as robotics, medical devices, automation systems, and aerospace equipment. By eliminating the frame, it enables improved thermal management, reduced weight, and flexible design customization.

        Key Features

        • High torque density with compact and lightweight design
        • Direct integration into machine assembly for space optimization
        • Precise speed and position control with smooth performance
        • High efficiency and low heat generation during operation
        • Flexible design enabling custom shafts, housings, and cooling solutions
        • Engineered for reliable, long-life performance in demanding environments
