        Agriculture

        Mecwin offers a reliable range of motors, pumps, and controllers designed to power modern agricultural equipment. From solar DC and AC pumps to high-torque motors for EV tillers, weeders, grass cutters, and tractors, our solutions deliver efficiency, durability, and performance in demanding field conditions. Built with advanced technology, Mecwin products support sustainable and energy-efficient farming for both indoor and outdoor applications.
